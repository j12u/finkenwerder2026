Title: Leyla Yenirce

----

Text:

(*1992 in Qubînê, Kurdistan)

In her artistic practice, Leyla Yenirce engages in a multimedia exploration of representations of resistance as well as cultural, media, and military structures of dominance. To this end, she collects both digital and analog image material, which she incorporates into her interdisciplinary works in ways that range from subtle to more explicit.

Her image archive bears witness to the widespread circulation of visual representations of women in resistance, while also reflecting her own media-shaped relationship to her place of birth, Kurdistan.

In her compositions, elaborate installations, video works, performances, and paintings, Yenirce skillfully employs techniques of sampling, layering, and collage, responding directly to the material she works with. She is described as a cultural theorist, filmmaker, musician, painter, performer, and installation artist—an artistic practice in which these different media converge into a multifaceted whole.
