Description: Being Strong Is Hard presents found footage of Kurdish female freedom fighters, activists, and journalists, intercut in rapid succession with pixelated landscape imagery and accompanied by a tense, abrasive electronic beat. In doing so, the artist relentlessly confronts the viewer with a central question: how can resistance be sustained against military and media dominance, and how can freedom be defended under such conditions? The women depicted by Leyla Yenirce have made their choice. The difficulty of this decision, along with all its implications, is immediately palpable.
