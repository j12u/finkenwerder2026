Description:

At the center of Contained Measures of a Kolanut lies an engagement with a natural resource that has been transformed into a global commodity. The kola nut plant was a key ingredient in Coca-Cola and other caffeinated soft drinks that entered the market around the turn of the 20th century. 
In Nigeria, the kola nut continues to hold diverse cultural and social significance: it is used in spiritual contexts, plays an important role in ceremonies, and serves as a means of fostering community and connection. By focusing on an immediate encounter with a single resource, Nkanga also draws attention to the complex histories of trade and power intertwined with it. In doing so, she shifts the focus toward the material realities of a globalized present, which are often described solely in abstract economic terms
