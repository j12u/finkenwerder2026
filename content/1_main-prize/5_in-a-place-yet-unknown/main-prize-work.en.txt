Title: In A Place Yet Unknown

----

Images: 

----

Year: 2017

----

Material: Woven textile (yarns: polyester, cotton, rubber Verdi, viscose, linen, acryl), copper metal container, ink, dyes, tapestry 266 x 180 cm, base 10 x 200 x 20 cm

----

Description: Woven into this tapestry is a poem by the artist on transformation. The lower part of the textile is immersed in black ink contained in a small metal basin. Gradually, the liquid rises through the fabric, altering its colour and slowly causing the woven poem to disappear. This material process of transformation becomes a metaphor for the ongoing change of societies, for the tension between continuity and the erosion of values, as well as for the capacity to generate new forms of life and coexistence.
