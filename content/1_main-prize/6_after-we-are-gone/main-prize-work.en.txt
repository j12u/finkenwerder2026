Title: After We Are Gone

Images: 

----

Year: 2020

----

Material: Woven textile (yarns: cotton, techno, cashmere, wool, trevira, sidero, pol- yester, elirex, mesh), 250 x 180 cm.

----

Description: The tapestry depicts a hybrid plant—an amalgam of three different plant forms layered one above the other in drawing, appearing like a genetically modified species. Set against the backdrop of the devastating wildfires of 2019/20 in California, the Amazon region, Australia, numerous parts of Africa, and Germany, a burned landscape unfolds behind the plant. Nkanga thus imagines a world after human presence, in which vegetation gradually reclaims the devastated terrain. The marks on the leaves point to the traces of destruction: the plant is still scarred, yet already in the process of regeneration.
