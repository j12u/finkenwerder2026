Description: Usakos is a small town on the historic railway line between Swakopmund and Tsumeb in Namibia. The railway line was originally built to transport ore from the Tsumeb mine to the coast. Nkanga documents the dilapidated ruins of the railway’s old main workshops. For her, these abandoned places symbolise the imperial past, when raw materials were extracted and shipped away to fuel prosperity in Europe.
