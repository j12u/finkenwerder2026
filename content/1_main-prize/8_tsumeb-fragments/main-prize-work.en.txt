Description:

With the modular installation Tsumeb Fragments, Otobong Nkanga unfolds a multilayered narrative around The Green Hill copper mine in Tsumeb, Namibia. The work draws on memories, research, and
collected materials connected to this site, which are reassembled in order to reveal intuitive and often invisible relationships. The original hill was irreversibly transformed into a crater after the
Ovambo people’s sustainable extraction of minerals for local use was displaced by industrial mining under German colonial rule. The accompanying video documents a performance by Nkanga, conceived in remembrance of the site’s former abundance of natural resources. Through song, she addresses the devastated landscape in a gesture of healing, consolation, and commemoration.
