Description: Kolanut Tales – Dismembered presents, in the form of a botanical depiction, the individual components of the kola nut plant: branches, leaves, blossoms, fruiting bodies, seeds, and germinating seedlings. Nkanga is interested in the methods through which plants and minerals have been studied and classified, and in the ways in which these systems of ordering generate new narratives
